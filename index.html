The Kitchen

Late hours,
steady wok,
warm bag at the door.

We're tucked into the Oakfield Centre on Rad Valley Road — close enough to Copthorne that the regulars know to call ahead by half past five. Anglo-Chinese cooking done the way you remember it: generous portions, the same dishes that have been working for years, plus a few that quietly improved without anyone noticing.

Phone the order through, give us a name and a postcode if you want it delivered, and we'll have it ready when you arrive — or at your door, hot, in the same window.
